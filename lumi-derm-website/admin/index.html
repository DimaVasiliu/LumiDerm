    Everything you can do here, in plain language. Use this guide when you want to change the website, send an email, or manage subscribers.

    The big picture

    This admin page is where you manage the parts of the website that change often: offers, prices, reviews, images, page text, subscribers and email campaigns.

    Most edits are saved in this browser first. They are not visible to clients until you click the matching Publish button for that section.

    Good to know: after publishing, give the website about a minute to update, then refresh the public page to check it.

    Regular admin to-do list

    Use this as a simple routine for keeping the website and email list clean.

    Quick checks
    • Open first to see anything that needs attention.
    • Check for new confirmed sign-ups.
    • If someone asks to be removed, delete them from Subscribers before sending any campaign.
    Before sending email
    • Give the campaign a clear name so you can find it later.
    • Choose the right audience and only send to people who gave marketing consent.
    • Check the live recipient count. If the number looks wrong, stop and reload before sending.
    • Send a test to yourself first and check it on your phone.
    • Check spelling, images, offer wording, button link and unsubscribe text.
    • Save the campaign draft before sending, so you can reuse or review it later.
    Website updates
    • Review homepage offers and remove anything expired or unclear.
    • Keep prices and offer wording consistent with what clients can actually book.
    • Use for About updates, homepage hero copy, Treatments intro, Booking intro and contact details.
    • Update images only when they improve the page or replace something old.
    • After publishing, open the live website and check the public page.
    Clean-up
    • Review saved campaign drafts and delete anything you will not reuse.
    • Export confirmed subscribers if you want a backup copy.
    • Check recent sends so you know what clients have already received.
    • Keep the website simple: only publish changes that are accurate and useful for clients.

    Best habit: make small, accurate updates. Clear offers and useful emails work better than changing too much at once.

    Offers — the cards on your homepage

    The Offers tab controls the treatment/offer cards visitors see on the homepage.

    • Add an offer: click Add offer, fill in the boxes, then Publish offers when it is ready.
    • Edit an offer: click it in the list and change the boxes. Save changes keeps it in this browser; Publish offers puts it live.
    • Delete an offer: open it and use delete — it disappears from the website too once published.
    • When you’re happy, click Publish offers. Live in about a minute.

    What each box means:

    • Title — the big name, e.g. “15% off your first visit”.
    • Category — the small label above the title, e.g. “New client offer”.
    • Description — a sentence or two explaining the offer.
    • Price — e.g. “From £70”, “£90”, or “Save up to 30%”.
    • Badge — the little tag on the picture, e.g. “Trial”, “New”, “Save 15%”.
    • Image — pick one from the list, or upload your own photo.
    • Links to treatment — choose the matching treatment so the “Book” button takes people straight to it.
    • Status — Live shows it; Draft hides it while you work on it.
    • End date — optional. After this date the offer disappears from the website by itself.
    • Featured — tick to make it the big highlighted card at the top.

    Tip: not sure about an offer yet? Set it to Draft — it saves but stays hidden from the public until you switch it to Live.

    Pages — website words you can change

    The Pages tab controls the safer text areas that change from time to time. These edits save in this browser first, then go live when you click Publish page text.

    • Homepage hero — the main headline and supporting line on the homepage.
    • Contact details — phone, email, address and social links. These update everywhere they appear on the website.
    • About page — hero text, About me paragraphs, clinic intro/cards, section headings and the final call-to-action.
    • Treatments page intro — the top text above the treatment menu. Prices themselves stay in the Prices tab.
    • Booking page intro — the top booking text, treatment chooser copy, calendar intro and help strip.

    About paragraphs: put a blank line between paragraphs in the About box. The website will turn each one into a separate paragraph.

    Still managed by Dima: legal pages, privacy/cookie/terms wording and detailed clinical treatment descriptions. Those can create compliance or safety issues if changed casually.

    Send email — message your subscribers

    This is how you email offers and news to people who joined your list. It’s three simple steps.

    1. Step 1 — Who: leave it on “My website subscribers” and your confirmed sign-ups load automatically — no files, no exporting. Want a smaller group? Use the Narrow it down boxes to pick people by treatment interest or birthday month. A live count shows exactly how many people will get it.
    2. Step 2 — Write: give the campaign a name (just for you), pick a template, edit the words, and tick which offers to include. Type {{name}} and it becomes each person’s first name.
    3. Step 3 — Send: always send a test to yourself first and check it on your phone. Then tick the confirmation box and click Send campaign. To send later, tick Schedule for later, pick a date and time, and it goes out automatically — see and cancel anything waiting under Upcoming scheduled sends.

    Automatic birthday emails: under Automatic birthday emails, write the message once and flip it On. Everyone with a birthday on file gets it automatically on their day (once a year, only if they consented). Use Send me a preview to see it first.

    Save it for later: click Save current under “Saved campaigns” to keep a campaign (words, offers and the group you chose). Reuse it anytime with Load — perfect for a monthly newsletter or a birthday email.

    Emailing existing Treatwell clients instead? Switch Step 1 to Treatwell client export and drop in the CSV from Treatwell — or just use Treatwell’s own Marketing tool for those. Your website subscribers are the ones that live here.

    Important: everyone in “My website subscribers” already confirmed and agreed — that’s handled for you. If you ever use a Treatwell CSV, only email people who ticked the marketing box.

    Subscribers — people who joined from the website

    When someone signs up through the pop-up on your website, they appear here after they confirm by email.

    • Confirmed = they clicked the confirmation email and can be emailed.
    • Pending = they signed up but haven’t confirmed yet, so don’t email them.
    • Export confirmed (CSV) downloads your consented sign-ups, ready to drop into the Send email tab.
    • Delete removes someone forever — use this if a person ever asks to be removed.

    Golden rules

    • Nothing here can break the website. Explore freely.
    • Publish, then wait a minute. Changes aren’t live until you click Publish, and it takes about a minute to appear.
    • Test emails on yourself first — every single time.
    • Only email people who agreed. The tools keep you safe if you let them.
    • Keep legal and treatment-safety wording out of quick edits. Ask Dima before changing policy pages or detailed clinical descriptions.
    • If a photo looks missing right after publishing, wait a minute and refresh — it’s just catching up.

    If something doesn’t look right

    • Change didn’t appear? Did you click Publish? Then wait a minute and refresh the website page (hold Shift and click reload).
    • “Publishing failed”? Try Publish again — it usually works on the second try. If it keeps failing, tell Dima.
    • Email won’t send? Check the Send email tab has a green “Ready” and that you ticked the confirmation box.
    • Still stuck? Nothing you did is broken — just message Dima and describe what you clicked.
