Laser & hair removal · Cynosure Elite

Laser hair removal at Lumi Derm uses the medical-grade Cynosure Elite system to target the pigment in each hair follicle with precisely controlled pulses of light, gradually reducing regrowth so skin stays smoother for far longer than waxing or shaving. The Elite platform is trusted, effective and suitable for a wide range of skin tones.

Because hair grows in cycles, a course of sessions is needed to catch every follicle in its active growth phase. Most clients book a course of 6 or 10 sessions spaced a few weeks apart, and the price per session reduces as the course length increases. Package discounts also apply when you combine multiple areas (see below).

What to expect: sessions are quick and most people feel only a mild, warm "elastic snap" sensation. You can return to normal activities straight away — simply avoid sun exposure, hot baths and tanning for a day or two. A patch test 24 hours beforehand confirms the treatment is right for your skin.

Apilus electrolysis is the only method of truly permanent hair removal, and the perfect partner to laser. A very fine probe delivers a tiny current to each individual follicle, permanently disabling its ability to grow hair — so it works beautifully on fine, light, red or grey hairs and small, precise areas that laser cannot target.

Because each follicle is treated individually, electrolysis suits every skin and hair type and is ideal for finishing touches, stubborn areas and delicate spots. Sessions are booked by time — 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes — so treatment can be as brief or as thorough as you need.

What to expect: a series of sessions is needed to clear an area permanently, as hair grows in cycles. A patch test is required 24 hours before your first appointment.

Injectables & regeneration

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) is a natural, regenerative treatment that harnesses your own biology. A small blood sample is taken and spun in a centrifuge to concentrate the platelets and growth factors, which are then reintroduced into the skin or scalp to stimulate repair, collagen production and circulation.

For the face and body, PRP improves skin quality, glow, firmness and fine lines. For the scalp, it supports thicker, healthier hair and can help slow shedding. Because it uses your own plasma it is extremely well tolerated, and a course of 3–5 sessions delivers the most visible, lasting results.

What to expect: mild redness or small pinpoint marks that settle within a day or two. A £10 consultation confirms suitability and is redeemable against your treatment.
