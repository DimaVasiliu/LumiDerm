Cookie Policy

How this website stores your choice and controls optional external content.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by websites. They can support essential website operation, remember your preferences, measure traffic or connect third-party services.

Cookies and similar technologies we use

  • Essential choice record — this site stores consent record version 3, your selected categories and a timestamp in local storage.
  • Online booking (Treatwell) — the official embedded booking widget is not contacted on page load. It loads only after you allow external content or select the one-time booking load button. An external Treatwell booking link remains available.
  • Maps (Google) — Google Maps is not contacted on page load. It loads only after you allow external content or select the one-time map load button.
  • Analytics — no analytics script is currently included in the website code.

Your choices

On your first visit, choose Allow external media or Essential only for optional Treatwell and Google content. You can also load either service once without changing the saved category. Use the permanent Cookie settings button in the footer to change your saved choice later.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete storage in your browser settings. External Treatwell booking and Google Maps directions remain available through clear links when embedded content is not loaded. Treatwell and Google's controls apply after their content is opened.
