About me

Clinically minded care, delivered calmly

I founded Lumi Derm Aesthetics to offer advanced skincare the way I believe it should be — unhurried, personalised and genuinely honest. Over the past decade I've trained across laser, injectable, regenerative and body treatments, and I only work with medical-grade technology I trust.

Every appointment begins with a real conversation. I take the time to understand your skin, your goals and your lifestyle, then recommend only what will actually help — never more than you need. If a treatment isn't right for you, I'll tell you honestly.

My aim is simple: results that look like you, delivered in a calm, private setting where you never feel rushed.

How I can help

The concerns I help you solve

Whatever's bothering you about your skin, hair or body, there's usually a considered, evidence-led way to improve it. These are the concerns I help with most often.

Unwanted hair

Endless shaving, waxing & ingrown hairs

Constant regrowth, irritation and ingrowns are exhausting to manage. I reduce hair long-term — and permanently where it's suitable.

How I help: Cynosure Elite laser & Apilus electrolysis

Dull & tired skin

Skin that's lost its glow

Dullness, rough texture and congestion make skin look tired. I resurface, brighten and rebalance for a healthy, lit-from-within finish.

How I help: iS Clinical facials, peels & microneedling

Lines & firmness

Early lines & loss of firmness

As collagen slows, skin can feel less bouncy and defined. I bio-remodel and hydrate from within to firm and smooth — naturally.

How I help: Profhilo, polynucleotides & skin boosters

Tone & redness

Pigmentation, redness & thread veins

Uneven tone, sun spots and visible vessels draw the eye. I target them precisely to even out and calm the complexion.

How I help: Laser skin rejuvenation & vascular

Hair loss

Thinning hair & shedding

Noticing more scalp or slower growth? I use regenerative treatments to nourish the follicles and support stronger, fuller hair.

How I help: PRP, mesotherapy & polynucleotides

Puffiness & contour

Puffiness & sluggish circulation

Fluid retention and heaviness can leave you feeling less sculpted. I stimulate drainage to de-puff, smooth and refine face and body.

How I help: Endospheres therapy & body treatments
