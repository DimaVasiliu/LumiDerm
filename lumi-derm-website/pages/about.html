About me

Clinically minded care, delivered calmly

I founded Lumi Derm Aesthetics to offer advanced skincare the way I believe it should be: unhurried, personalised and honest. Over the past decade I've trained across laser, injectable, regenerative and body treatments, and I work with technology and products I trust.

Every appointment begins with a real conversation. I take time to understand your skin, your goals and your lifestyle, then recommend what is likely to help and what is worth leaving alone. If a treatment is not right for you, I will say so.

The aim is not to change your face or chase trends. It is to support healthier-looking skin, soften what bothers you and help you feel more comfortable in your own skin.

IuliaFounder & Lead Practitioner, Lumi Derm Aesthetics

The clinic

A quiet Docklands studio for skin, laser and body care

Lumi Derm Aesthetics is a modern, private treatment space in London Docklands. The menu is broad, but the approach is simple: choose the treatment that fits the concern, explain it clearly, and keep each appointment focused on the person in front of me.

What I offer

Facials, chemical peels, skin boosters, mesotherapy, PRP, laser hair removal, vascular laser treatments, electrolysis for hair and blemish removal, lymphatic drainage and Endospheres therapy.

How to get here

South Quay and Crossharbour stations are both about a 4-minute walk away. If you are driving, there is free and paid parking nearby, including ASDA parking around 10 minutes away on foot.

What matters

Appointments are one-to-one, treatment plans are tailored, and product choices are considered, with cruelty-free options used where they fit the treatment.

How I can help

The concerns I help you solve

Most clients come in with something specific they want to improve: hair growth, texture, dullness, redness, thinning hair, puffiness or skin that no longer feels like theirs. These are the concerns I help with most often.

Unwanted hair

Endless shaving, waxing & ingrown hairs

Constant regrowth, irritation and ingrowns are exhausting to manage. I reduce hair long-term — and permanently where it's suitable.

How I help: Cynosure Elite laser & Apilus electrolysis

Dull & tired skin

Skin that's lost its glow

Dullness, rough texture and congestion can make skin look tired. I resurface, brighten and rebalance with treatments chosen around your skin tolerance.

How I help: iS Clinical facials, peels & microneedling

Lines & firmness

Early lines & loss of firmness

As collagen slows, skin can feel less bouncy and defined. I use hydration and bio-remodelling treatments to support smoother, fresher-looking skin.

How I help: Profhilo, polynucleotides & skin boosters

Tone & redness

Pigmentation, redness & thread veins

Uneven tone, sun spots and visible vessels can be distracting. I target suitable concerns carefully, with patch tests and realistic expectations.

How I help: Laser skin rejuvenation & vascular

Hair loss

Thinning hair & shedding

Noticing more scalp or slower growth? I use regenerative treatments to nourish the follicles and support stronger, fuller hair.

How I help: PRP, mesotherapy & polynucleotides

Puffiness & contour

Puffiness & sluggish circulation

Fluid retention and heaviness can affect how the face and body feel. I use drainage-focused treatments to support a lighter, smoother appearance.

How I help: Endospheres therapy & body treatments
